Open a folder. It plays.

FolderVideoPlayer plays the videos on your NAS straight on your Apple TV. No media server, no account, no subscription. The folder is the playlist.

Free until 31 Oct 2026, then $1.99 once. No subscription, ever.

No media server

Nothing to install on the NAS. The app talks straight to your SMB share: Synology, QNAP, Windows, macOS, Linux, or a folder your Mac already shares.

Fast with 5,000+ videos

Every thumbnail is made once and kept, on the TV and beside your files, so browsing a huge library stays instant.

Nothing leaves home

No accounts, analytics, ads or cloud. The only thing the app talks to is your share, on your own network.

Set up in a minute

  1. Open the appNo sign-up. It looks for shares on your network straight away.
  2. Pick your shareChoose it from the list or type its address. Your password stays in the tvOS Keychain.
  3. Press playPlay a whole folder in one go, or browse it sorted by name, date or size.

Tag it while you watch

Open the tag panel mid-video and the picture waits for you. Find videos later by what you remember, not by a file name you forgot.

Tags are saved in a small .FolderVideoPlayer folder on your own share, so every Apple TV and Mac pointed at it sees the same ones. No sync, no account.

Picks up where you stopped

Quit halfway, come back days later, and it offers to carry on from the exact spot, even in a 40-hour archive.

Manage the library on your Mac. Free.

The Mac app reads the same share. Tag in bulk from the keyboard, find duplicates, and let on-device AI suggest tags. Everything you do shows up on the TV.
