Made for the living room

Everything a folder of videos deserves on a TV

No transcoding servers, no photo-library tax, no “supported cloud only”. Just your files, presented properly.

Browse any SMB share

Works with Synology, QNAP, Windows, macOS, Linux — anything that speaks SMB. Find your share by name over Bonjour or type its address. Folders, sort, tags, and all the deep paths you actually keep.

Smart thumbnail caching

Every thumbnail is generated once and never again — even in libraries of 5,000+ videos browsing stays fast. Frames are kept on the TV and alongside your files on the share, so nothing is rebuilt for a second device either.

Tags that travel with you

Tag your videos on the TV, see them on your Mac, iPhone or a second Apple TV. Tags live in a small folder on your own share — synced through your network, never through a server.

Picks up where you stopped

Quit mid-episode, come back days later — it offers to carry on from the exact spot. Play order and preferences are remembered on the device, so the TV behaves like yours.

Support

Questions, answered

What can FolderVideoPlayer play?
Anything your Apple TV can decode — MP4, MOV, MKV and more — from any SMB file share on your network. If the file plays in tvOS, it plays here.
Do I need an account, an internet connection or a NAS?
None of the three. There are no accounts and the app works fully offline on your local network. A NAS is the most common setup, but the share can live on a Mac, a Windows PC, a router with a USB drive — anything that serves SMB.
Will it slow down with a very large library?
No. Thumbnails are generated once and cached — on the TV and next to your files on the share — so browsing stays fast even in libraries of 5,000+ videos. A thumbnail that has been made is never made again.
Can I use the same tags on my Mac and my Apple TV?
Yes. Tags and poster frames are stored in a .FolderVideoPlayer folder on your own share, so every device pointed at the same share sees the same tags — and your Mac companion app can read and write them too.
